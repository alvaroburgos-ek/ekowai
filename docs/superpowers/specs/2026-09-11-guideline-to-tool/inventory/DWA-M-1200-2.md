## 5. Summary
Counts: 7 table-lookups / 7 repeatable groups / 13 conditionals / 4 equations.

Top 5 UX wins:
1. Class-driven validation: pick `wassergueteklasse` → `leistungsziel_log10` per organism, N=16 rule, percentile choice and "not applicable" for B-2/C-2/D all pre-filled.
2. Validation samples as 16 paired rows per organism with LRV_i, MW, SD, P10/P50 and the 15/16 or 8/16 verdict computed (replaces 12 hand-typed statistics fields).
3. Verfahrenskette as repeatable stages (enum from Tab. 6/B.2) that spawn the stage-specific design fields, Tab. 6 monitoring parameters and Tab. B.2 log10 credits — collapsing worksheets 09/10/11/13/20/21/23.
4. `desinfektionsverfahren` drives which dose/CT field is visible and which Tab. 4 validation proof is demanded.
5. Inherit class, aerosol/pasture flags and effluent values from DWA-M-1200-1 instead of re-typing.

Data-quality gaps: four empty worksheets (08, 20, 21, 23); `pfas_summe_20` unit µg/l vs ng/l in 1200-1 (Tab. 19 says "< 100 ng/l"); `verfahrenskette` is free text; `log10_reduktionen` json duplicates the `x_i/y_i/lrv_i` scalars; VR rules `rule:*` are opaque strings (limits not machine-readable); enum for `bewaesserungsmethode` inconsistent across the series; Tab. B.1/B.2 values absent from prod.
