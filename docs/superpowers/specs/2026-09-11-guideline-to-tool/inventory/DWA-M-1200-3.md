## 5. Summary
Counts: 9 table-lookups / 7 repeatable groups / 16 conditionals / 5 distinct equations (10 rows).

Top 5 UX wins:
1. Repeatable "Schlag/Fläche" rows (Fläche, Kultur, Pflanzentyp, Technik, Sprinkler, Wurfweite, Spritzschutz) that inherit `gueteklasse` and auto-fill Karenzzeit, Emitterabstand and Tab. 7/8/9 `faktor` → `min_abstand` per row — removes ~20 hand-typed fields.
2. Tab. 11 limits pre-filled and switched by `pflanzentyp`; measured values as dated sample rows with fracht Σ and the BBodSchV/3 check computed.
3. Collapse the 58-field M12003-05 mirror worksheet: every symbol already lives in its topical worksheet; one source each.
4. Tab. 3 / Tab. 13 / Tab. 14: pick `speichertyp` and `desinfektion_methode` → allowed classes, allowed application moments and dose (30 mg/l Cl, 0,1 ml/l H2O2, 12–24 h) filled, and CR-06 fixed to real enum values.
5. Bewässerungstagebuch as rows (Tab. 2 columns) feeding actual water use and heavy-metal load instead of a free json blob.

Data-quality gaps: CR-06/CR-06-2 compare `speichertyp` with 'geschlossen'/'offen' which are not enum values; 20 requirement rows are exact duplicates (`CR-xx` + `CR-xx-2`); 5 equations registered twice; `kultur_typ` is data_type text but carries enum_values (2 values only); `helminthen_eier` VR "<=1" vs Tab. 8 "≤ 1 Ei pro Liter für Weideflächen oder Futterpflanzen" (conditional, not global); `legionella_konz` VR "<1000 bei Sprinkler" not gated; M12003-02 and -25 empty; Tab. 15 and Tab. 12 content absent from prod.
