## 1. Table-lookup candidates

## 5. Summary
Counts: table-lookups 5 (all unit/structure lookups; 0 numeric-value tables) / repeatable groups 9 (1 wired; 2 dominant: KG item rows with Menge×Kennwert, stage×KG matrix) / conditionals 11 (4 encoded) / equations 53 present + ~18 missing sums.

TOP 5 UX wins:
1. Collapse the five stage worksheets (-18…-22, ~90 fields) into one stage×KG matrix whose row totals, `*_gesamt`, current/previous stage totals (-26) and deviations (-27) are derived — this removes the largest block of re-typed EUR values in the corpus.
2. Give every KG item a quantity + reference unit (from Tab. 2/3/4, filled by KG selection) and a Kennwert so `kg_NNN = Menge × Kennwert` is computed, with the EUR override audited; today only the EUR is typed and the tables' units are unused.
3. Delete the copy worksheets -17 and -25 (klassif_*, BKO_*) and the BU_* block in -06 in favour of inheritance from the equation outputs (`kg_N00_total`, `GK_total`, `building_costs`, DIN 277 quantities in -05) — 22 duplicate fields.
4. Sonderkosten register (Art enum from the wired `special_cost_flags`, KG, Betrag, Annahmen) driving REQ-10…14 and the 15 stage `*_anteil` fields.
5. Gate KG worksheets and stage worksheets on the wired checklists `applicable_cost_groups` / `planning_stage_active` and on `cost_breakdown_depth` (level 1/2/3) so a Kostenrahmen does not present 250 third-level fields.

Data-quality gaps: -24 fields `cost`, `reference_unit`, `cost_parameter` carry "(Engineer: Label/Einheit pruefen)" placeholders; unit strings inconsistent ("m²" vs "1" for GRZ/GFZ, "St." for count); REQ-09, REQ-26…29 have empty conditions; many `*_lph`, `*_kostenstatus`, `KK_stage_*`, `GK_quelle_stage`, `AA_typ` are free text where the description lists fixed options (should be enums per the owner ruling on selection widgets); Table 4 (KG 400 units) present in the transcript but no prod field references its rows.
