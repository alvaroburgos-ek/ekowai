> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** A138-10's `r_D_n` and `D_min` stop being free-typed and instead derive from the basin's governing-duration iteration; `Q_zu` (Gl.3) then auto-computes at the worst-case D.

**Architecture:** The basin Gl.8 engine (A138-13) already iterates and knows `governingD` + `r_D_at_governing` (`GoverningResult`). Expose those as two derived fields on A138-13 (`D_gov`, `r_D_gov`), materialized by the engine alongside `V_VA`; A138-10 inherits them (single producer) so `D_min`←`D_gov`, `r_D_n`←`r_D_gov`, and `Q_zu` computes via the unchanged Gl.3. No local iteration on A138-10 (would double-produce), no free-typing.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Next.js, Drizzle, Vitest, Supabase.
