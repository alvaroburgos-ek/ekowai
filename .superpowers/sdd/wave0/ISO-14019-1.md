## Findings (by defect class)

### DEAD GATE (1) — fix-first
- **CR-006** (Appropriate sampling, §4.2.3): `condition = "manual"` — a literal string, not a boolean
  expression. evaluate.ts cannot parse it → the gate **never fires a fail**. Field `sampling_applied`
  exists on WS-03 but is referenced by no CR. Ruling: [[dp-cr006-manual-deadgate]] — intentional
  manual-review sentinel (reclassify as non-enforcing) vs mis-encoded gate (fix to
  `sampling_applied == true`).

### VERDICT-AS-PRODUCER / effectively-dead (1) — fix-first
- **CR-024** (§6.2.2): `assurance_conclusion IN {'unmodified','qualified','adverse','disclaimed'}`
  enumerates the field's **entire** enum domain → true for every valid selection, fails only on NULL.
  A required-ness check disguised as a compliance decision; cannot discriminate adverse/disclaimed.
  §6.2.2 modal is "may". Ruling: [[dp-cr024-verdict-producer]].
