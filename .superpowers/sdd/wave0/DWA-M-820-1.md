## Tier rationale — fix-first
The encoding is structurally complete (25 procurement-phase worksheets, 26 gates, 81
fields, clean §-to-printed-page mapping) and the one DWA-original numeric table
(Tab. D.1) plus every §-anchor are in the readable PDF and VA-attested — so the
standard is **not acquisition-blocked for its own content** (there is no deferred DWA
sizing table to acquire; the 12 missing docs are restated law/EU context, `named` by
the gates rather than a DWA table the harness must read). But a harness run is not
meaningful until the gate layer is repaired: **0 equations** for a numeric-threshold
standard (all arithmetic lives in CR condition strings), **7 phantom-field
cross-worksheet gates**, **4 prose/always-true dead gates**, a **verdict-as-producer
self-gate** (REQ-25), and the D.1/EU-threshold **hand-enterable-derived** values.
These are DATA-track encoding defects fixable without new source (plus the 7-item
Alvaro decision batch). → **fix-first**.

Note vs sibling DWA-M-820-2 (fix-first, process-only, 0 equations by nature): Teil 1
is fix-first for a *different* reason — it genuinely needs numeric enforcement
(equations + worksheet-scope fixes), whereas Teil 2 had nothing to compute. The clean
process gates here (REQ-02/03/10/16/17/23/26 — plain booleans on their own worksheet)
DO fire and are honest.
