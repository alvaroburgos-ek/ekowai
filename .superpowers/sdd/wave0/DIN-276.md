## Tier rationale — fix-first
Not harness-ready: 21 dead gates mean the compliance layer is largely non-enforcing as encoded
(attestation checkboxes + phantom predicates). These are DATA-track fixes (bind conditions to real
fields / pair attestations with substantive checks / relocate cross-worksheet gates) that must land
before a harness run is meaningful. Not acquisition-blocked overall: the standard's VA spine (Tables
1–4 location, KG-aggregation equations, §3.11/§3.12 identities) is fully mappable from the in-library
DIN 276 PDF; only the reference-unit *match* gates (REQ-27/28/29) and the cost-parameter denominator
are gated on DIN 277-1 acquisition — a bounded subset, not the whole standard.
