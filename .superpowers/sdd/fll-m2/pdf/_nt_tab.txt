                                                                                                                                                                          7        Quality requirements


                                                                                                                                                                          7.1      Requirements pertaining to water quality


                                                                                                                                                                          7.1.1    Fill-up water

                                                                                                                                                                          The fill-up water plays a very important role, both when initially filling up the pool and when
                                                                                                                                                                          water is topped up, e. g. in order to compensate for evaporation. Tap water, well water and
                                                                                                                                                                          rainwater are all potentially suitable as fill-up water.

                                                                                                                                                                          Due to the way drinking water is currently purified, it may already contain such high phosphorus
                                                                                                                                                                          levels that it is no longer suitable as fill-up water unless further purification takes place. Fur-
                                                                                                                                                                          thermore, the phosphorus content may vary during the course of the year.

                                                                                                                                                                          Well water can contain increased nutrient contents, e.g. due to agricultural runoffs or high
                                                                                                                                                                          contents of iron or other substances.

                                                                                                                                                                          Rainwater cannot be introduced directly because of the atmospheric substance deposition,
                                                                                                                                                                          e.g. on roofs. Furthermore, water should not be used if larger amounts of metal (zinc, copper)
                                                                                                                                                                          were used because these lead to an increased concentration of heavy metals in the water. If
                                                                                                                                                                          water is taken from cisterns, floating discharge is recommended.

                                                                                                                                                                          Surface water from rivers and lakes cannot be introduced.

                                                                                                                                                                          The suitability of fill-up water must always be checked. In some cases, a more extensive water
                                                                                                                                                                          purification process is required.
