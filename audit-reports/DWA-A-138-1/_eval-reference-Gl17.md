# Eval-Reference — DWA-A 138-1, A138-18, Gl. 17 (A_S,m Rigole)

`A_S,m = (b_R + h_R) · L_R + b_R · h_R` — Versickerungsfläche der Rigole, §6.4.2.

| Input | Value | Unit |
|---|---:|---|
| b_R | 1.0 | m |
| h_R | 1.0 | m |
| L_R | 10 | m |

A_S,m = (1 + 1) · 10 + 1 · 1 = 20 + 1 = **21.000 m²**.

Precision ± 1 × 10⁻⁹. All three inputs must carry `m` (per §6.4.2 L1828-29). Mismatch → manual_required.
