# Eval-Reference — DWA-A 138-1, A138-12, Gl. 4 (Q_S)

`Q_S = k_i · A_S · 10³` — Versickerungsleistung in l/s, §5.3.3.6.

| Input | Value | Unit |
|---|---:|---|
| k_i | 5 × 10⁻⁵ | m/s |
| A_S | 100 | m² |

Q_S = 5×10⁻⁵ · 100 · 10³ = **5.000 l/s**.

Precision ± 1 × 10⁻⁹. Unit guard: k_i must be `m/s`; A_S must be `m²`. Mismatch → manual_required.
